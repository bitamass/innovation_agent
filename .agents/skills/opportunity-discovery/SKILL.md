# Opportunity Discovery

## Purpose

Identify meaningful innovation opportunities and present them to the Chief of Staff Agent for further evaluation.

## Inputs

Use the information available from the request, including:

- Organizational goals
- Problem or area of interest
- Stakeholders or users
- Current processes
- Known pain points
- Available evidence
- Constraints

If important information is missing, state what is missing and ask focused questions when necessary.

## Workflow

1. Clarify the problem, objective, or area to explore.
2. Identify the affected users and stakeholders.
3. Examine relevant internal patterns, external trends, and existing alternatives.
4. Identify unmet needs, inefficiencies, and opportunity gaps.
5. Generate multiple potential opportunity statements.
6. Compare the opportunities based on relevance, potential value, urgency, and available evidence.
7. Recommend which opportunities should proceed to formal assessment.

## Required Output

Provide:

- Executive summary
- Problem or focus area
- Stakeholders affected
- Relevant trends or observations
- Unmet needs and pain points
- Candidate opportunities
- Supporting evidence
- Assumptions and information gaps
- Recommended opportunities for assessment
- Proposed next actions

## Handoffs

Send promising opportunities to the Opportunity Assessment skill.

When additional support is required, recommend that the Chief of Staff Agent assign:

- Quantitative evidence questions to the Data Analyst Agent
- Regulatory or policy questions to the Compliance Agent
- Implementation planning to the Project Management Agent

## Guardrails

- Do not invent trends, stakeholder feedback, or supporting evidence.
- Clearly separate confirmed information from assumptions.
- Do not recommend implementation before the opportunity is evaluated.
- Present multiple reasonable opportunities when the evidence supports them.
